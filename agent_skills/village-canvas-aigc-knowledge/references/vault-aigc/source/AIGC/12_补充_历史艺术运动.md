# 全球视觉风格补遗 - 历史艺术运动

## 古代艺术（公元前3000年-公元5世纪）
- 古埃及艺术 | Ancient Egyptian art, hieroglyphic, profile view, canonical, monumental
- 古希腊艺术 | Ancient Greek art, classical, idealized, marble, pottery, column
- 古罗马艺术 | Ancient Roman art, realistic portrait, mosaic, fresco, arch, dome
- 拜占庭艺术 | Byzantine art, religious icon, gold background, mosaic, hierarchical

## 中世纪艺术（5-15世纪）
- 罗马式艺术 | Romanesque art, thick walls, rounded arches, religious, fortress-like
- 哥特式艺术 | Gothic art, pointed arch, stained glass, verticality, religious, flying buttress
- 国际哥特式 | International Gothic, elegant, courtly, detailed, 14-15th century
- 中世纪手稿 | Medieval manuscript, illuminated, religious text, gold leaf, marginalia

## 文艺复兴（14-17世纪）
- 早期文艺复兴 | Early Renaissance, linear perspective, humanism, Italy, Florence
- 北方文艺复兴 | Northern Renaissance, oil painting, detail, Flanders, domestic interior
- 样式主义 | Mannerism, elongated figures, artificial poses, tension, anti-classical
- 威尼斯画派 | Venetian school, colorito, rich color, Titian, Veronese, Tintoretto

## 17-18世纪
- 巴洛克 | Baroque, dramatic lighting, movement, emotion, grandeur, Caravaggio, Bernini
- 洛可可 | Rococo, pastel colors, playful, ornate, aristocratic, frivolous, Watteau
- 荷兰黄金时代 | Dutch Golden Age, realism, domestic, Vermeer, Rembrandt, still life
- 西班牙黄金时代 | Spanish Golden Age, religious, Velázquez, Zurbarán, Murillo
- 新古典主义 | Neoclassicism, return to classical, moral virtue, restrained, David, Ingres

## 19世纪
- 浪漫主义 | Romanticism, emotion, nature, sublime, individualism, Delacroix, Turner
- 现实主义 | Realism, everyday life, working class, unidealized, Courbet, Millet
- 前拉斐尔派 | Pre-Raphaelite, medieval revival, detailed, literary subjects, Rossetti, Millais
- 印象派 | Impressionism, visible brushstrokes, light study, Monet, plein air, soft focus
- 后印象派 | Post-Impressionism, structural, emotional, Van Gogh, Cézanne, Gauguin
- 点彩派 | Pointillism, scientific color, dots, Seurat, Signac, Neo-Impressionism
- 象征主义 | Symbolism, dream, mystical, Moreau, Redon, mythological, allegorical
- 纳比派 | Nabis, decorative, flat color, Bonnard, Vuillard, intimate interior
- 拉斐尔前派 | Aestheticism, art for art's sake, beautiful, decorative, Whistler

## 20世纪早期（1900-1945）
- 新艺术运动 | Art Nouveau, organic lines, floral, whiplash curve, Mucha, Klimt
- 装饰艺术 | Art Deco, geometric, streamlined, luxury, modern, 1920s-1940s
- 野兽派 | Fauvism, bold color, emotional, Matisse, Derain, non-naturalistic
- 表现主义 | Expressionism, distorted forms, emotional color, Munch, Kirchner, Die Brücke
- 青骑士 | Der Blaue Reiter, spiritual, abstract, Kandinsky, Marc, Russian
- 立体主义 | Cubism, geometric fragmentation, multiple perspectives, Picasso, Braque
- 未来主义 | Futurism, speed, technology, violence, movement, Boccioni, Balla
- 达达主义 | Dada, anti-art, absurd, collage, readymade, Duchamp, Hausmann
- 构成主义 | Constructivism, geometric, industrial, Russian, revolutionary, Tatlin, Rodchenko
- 风格派 | De Stijl, primary colors, grid, Mondrian, Dutch, neoplasticism
- 包豪斯 | Bauhaus, form follows function, geometric, modernist, Gropius, Albers
- 超现实主义 | Surrealism, dream logic, Dali, Magritte, impossible objects, subconscious
- 精确主义 | Precisionism, industrial, geometric, American, clean, Sheeler, Demuth
- 社会现实主义 | Social Realism, working class, political, American, Depression era
- 墨西哥壁画运动 | Mexican muralism, political, Rivera, Siqueiros, Orozco, public art

## 20世纪中期（1945-1970）
- 抽象表现主义 | Abstract Expressionism, action painting, color field, Pollock, Rothko
- 色域绘画 | Color Field painting, large areas of color, Rothko, Newman, contemplative
- 波普艺术 | Pop Art, mass culture, consumerism, Warhol, Lichtenstein, Hamilton
- 极简主义 | Minimalism, geometric, industrial, reductive, Judd, Andre, Stella
- 观念艺术 | Conceptual Art, idea over form, text, performance, Kosuth, Weiner
- 大地艺术 | Land Art, earthworks, natural materials, site-specific, Smithson, Heizer
- 行为艺术 | Performance Art, body, time, ephemeral, live, Abramović, Acconci
- 贫穷艺术 | Arte Povera, humble materials, Italian, anti-commercial, Merz, Pistoletto
- 光与空间运动 | Light and Space, perceptual, California, Turrell, Irwin, Bell
- 新现实主义 | Nouveau Réalisme, French, objects, Arman, Christo, Hains
- 硬边绘画 | Hard-edge painting, geometric, flat color, Kelly, Frankenthaler
- 欧普艺术 | Op Art, optical illusion, movement, Riley, Vasarely, perceptual

## 20世纪晚期（1970-2000）
- 新表现主义 | Neo-Expressionism, figurative, emotional, German/Italian, Baselitz, Kiefer
- 新几何 | Neo-Geo, simulation, appropriation, Jeff Koons, Sherrie Levine
- 英国YBA | Young British Artists, shock, conceptual, Damien Hirst, Emin, Ofili
- 关系美学 | Relational Aesthetics, social, participatory, interactive, Bourriaud
- 后现代主义 | Postmodernism, pastiche, irony, appropriation, Gehry, Venturi
- 新影像艺术 | New Media Art, digital, interactive, networked, Nam June Paik
- 装置艺术 | Installation Art, immersive, site-specific, environment, Kapoor, Eliasson
- 街头艺术 | Street Art, graffiti, urban, political, Banksy, Basquiat, Haring
- 新摄影 | New Photography, staged, conceptual, Jeff Wall, Cindy Sherman, Gursky
- 新现实主义 | Neo-Realism, contemporary, documentary, Chinese, Liu Xiaodong

- 关系美学2.0 | Relational Aesthetics 2.0, digital social, networked, participatory
- 后互联网艺术 | Post-Internet Art, digital native, online/offline, Artie Vierkant
